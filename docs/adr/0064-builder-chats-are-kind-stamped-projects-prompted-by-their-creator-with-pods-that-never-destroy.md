# Builder chats are kind-stamped Projects prompted by their creator, with pods that create and edit but never destroy

[ADR-0056](0056-builder-chats-are-hidden-projects-with-writes-resolved-to-the-launching-user.md) gave the Skill & Agent Builder a chassis — a `projects` row with `workspace_id` NULL, excluded from every listing — and a credential, a bespoke `gateway/store` controller family resolving a gateway key to the launching user. Five decisions since have removed most of what it rested on. [ADR-0059](0059-skills-exist-only-as-published-versions-and-a-built-in-agents-skills-live-on-a-development-profile.md) deleted the Skill draft tier, so "chat powers stop at drafts" now stops at nothing and the chat became the only place unfinished Skill work exists. [ADR-0058](0058-a-custom-agent-holds-one-definition-per-environment-and-a-project-keeps-the-files-it-was-created-with.md) gave agents no drafts either, only a Development profile that editing overwrites. [ADR-0061](0061-the-platform-harness-is-a-built-in-skill-and-an-agents-prompt-is-the-authors-body-alone.md) removed the harness an `agent-authoring` skill was meant to teach writing "on top of". [ADR-0062](0062-a-pod-calls-the-platform-api-as-the-human-who-created-its-project-bounded-to-what-it-created.md) built the general pod-caller mechanism the `gateway/store` family would now duplicate. [ADR-0063](0063-the-builder-tests-a-child-agent-by-conversing-with-it-in-one-throwaway-project.md) replaced in-session roleplay with a real test Project and noted that hiding it "rests entirely on the listing exclusion ADR-0056 must build".

**A Builder chat is a Project carrying a `kind` that both files it in the Builder's list and keeps it out of everyone else's; every permission holder sees the list, only its creator may prompt it; and its pod may create and edit but is refused every destructive verb.** Concretely:

- **One Built-in Agent, kind chosen at New chat.** `skill-agent-builder` is the second public entry in `agent-config/agents.json`, `poolSize` 0, carrying two authoring skills. "New Skill" and "New Agent" stamp `projects.kind`, which selects the opening authoring skill and the list facet — it never constrains what the chat may author, so an agent chat that needs a Skill mid-conversation simply makes one. There is no second Builder profile: [ADR-0059](0059-skills-exist-only-as-published-versions-and-a-built-in-agents-skills-live-on-a-development-profile.md) removed the agent-embedded Skill kind, leaving one artifact pipeline and no artifact-level reason for two prompts, two templates and two registry entries to version.
- **`kind` is the hiding discriminator, because nothing else on the row can be.** `projects` carries no visibility column: visibility is derived from `tenant_id`, `workspace_id` and `workspace_members` in `visibleProjectsFilter`. A Builder chat and a test Project are `workspace_id` NULL, which is the Organization-wide Public setting — the most visible one there is — so concealment is entirely a listing predicate, and `kind IS NULL` is that predicate. It covers both hidden kinds with one column, replacing the two different keys ADR-0056 and ADR-0063 each proposed. The predicate belongs in four query sites, not one: `visibleProjectsFilter` serves the app and public-API lists, while `findAll`, `selectProjects` and `findAllInWorkspace` are tenant-scoped with no visibility filter at all and leak Organization-wide if they reach a surface.
- **The list is shared; the transcript is not.** Every `can_manage_skills`/`can_manage_agents` holder sees every chat in the Organization as a row — title, creator, when, and the artifacts it produced — and only the creator may open or prompt it. The proxy's ensure hop gains a third predicate beside tenant membership and project visibility. This is what "any other user can do it as well" buys without moving an identity: chat access *is* the write credential's perimeter, and under [ADR-0062](0062-a-pod-calls-the-platform-api-as-the-human-who-created-its-project-bounded-to-what-it-created.md) whoever prompts a chat also commands its test Projects.
- **Chat powers are exactly the reversible acts.** In chat: create a Skill and publish new Versions of it, create a Custom Agent and edit its Development profile, add, remove and re-pin Skills on that profile, and create, run and delete its own test Project. Store UI only: **Publish (Agent)** to another Environment, widening a Skill or Agent to public, and Delete or Archive of any artifact. Artifacts the Builder creates are private to the launching user. The line is recoverability — every chat act can be undone by doing it again, while the excluded three are irreversible ([ADR-0057](0057-authored-skills-and-agents-are-private-or-public-within-one-organization.md) makes widening one-way) or destroy an Environment's content with no copy kept ([ADR-0058](0058-a-custom-agent-holds-one-definition-per-environment-and-a-project-keeps-the-files-it-was-created-with.md)), and neither renders its consequences in a chat bubble.
- **The pod is bounded by verb, where a Project is bounded by object.** ADR-0062 contains a pod by *which* Projects it may touch and grants full creator authority within them. Skills and Agents invert it: the pod may reach every artifact its creator may reach, and is refused Delete, Archive and widen-to-public at the guard regardless of what that human holds. The asymmetry is the blast radius — a Project is cheap and re-creatable, a Skill Version that agents across the Organization pin is not — and a Builder chat is where untrusted text routinely lands, as pasted documentation, fetched pages and imported `.skill` archives. Ownership-scoping artifacts the way Projects are scoped was rejected because "Edit with Builder" opens a *new* chat on an artifact an earlier chat or a colleague authored.
- **Those routes ride `/api/v1`, not a controller family of their own.** ADR-0062's `CallerSource: 'gateway-key'` on `PublicApiGuard` with `@AllowsGatewayCaller()` default-deny is the mechanism; the Builder's needs are a second opt-in group — Skills, Agent profiles, profile Skills — beside its seven Project routes, with the destructive verbs simply absent from the list. ADR-0056's `gateway/store` family is retired unbuilt.
- **Archive is `disabled`, Delete is hard, and neither is timed.** A chat lives until a human acts: no TTL and no reaper, matching ADR-0063's rejection of timed expiry. `ProjectService.disable` already keeps the row and the workspace files while destroying the pods, which is precisely "archive a chat" and costs only storage; `ProjectService.remove` cascades, tombstones the directory for the retention window, and takes the chat's live test Project with it. Deleting a chat that has published nothing warns, names what is lost, and offers archive in the same dialog — it does not refuse, because most abandoned chats are junk and a delete that cannot be performed is a list that only grows.
- **Provenance is one nullable column per artifact table.** `skills` and `agents` gain `created_in_chat_project_id`, stamped by the gateway caller at creation and never updated by later edits, mirroring `projects.created_by_project_id`. It is what the shared list shows and what "open the chat that made this" follows. A Skill that is published and then archived leaves its chat entirely alone: the chat is provenance, not lifecycle, and stays usable to publish a fresh Version.
- **What ships in the image.** `agent.md` is short — who the Builder is and the shape of the conversation — because [ADR-0061](0061-the-platform-harness-is-a-built-in-skill-and-an-agents-prompt-is-the-authors-body-alone.md) puts facts in skills. Three skills: `platform`, which the Builder needs not to build an app but to teach those facts into a child's prompt and to read the observability schemas ADR-0063 moved there; `skill-authoring`, the skill-creator methodology port — when a Skill is warranted, the frontmatter contract, the description as the trigger, when a fact belongs in `references/`, and the ERRORS/LINTS split from the validation research; and `agent-authoring`, carrying ADR-0061's four clauses and ADR-0063's test contract with its four caveats. The template has no `app/` and no go-live: a scratch directory where Skill files are composed before publishing, the offline validator so the error loop is local, and the `.skill` packager.
- **The test loop is documented shell, not a CLI.** `agent-authoring` holds a `references/` file with the exact calls. Its create recipe leads with the delete of the predecessor, so ADR-0063's one-live-per-chat rule is a written step; readiness is a single blocking `until … sleep` line rather than a poll per turn; and because ADR-0062's allow-list includes listing the Projects the pod itself created, a resumed chat asks which test Project is its own instead of remembering, and cleans up leftovers.

## Considered options

- **Two Built-in Agents, `skill-builder` and `agent-builder`** — rejected: with no agent-embedded Skill kind there is one pipeline, so the split buys a list facet at the price of two prompts, two templates, two registry versions, and a wall between authoring an agent and inventing a Skill for it mid-conversation. A `kind` column buys the same facet for one nullable column.
- **A read-only transcript for other holders** — deferred, not rejected. The transcript lives in opencode's database inside the pod, so a colleague browsing a suspended chat pays a cold boot and keep-alive, and the chat proxy needs a GET-only mode that is a new mechanism rather than a predicate on an existing one. The browsing value — *that* a colleague built this and *what* it produced — is delivered by the metadata list and the provenance column at no such cost.
- **Resuming another's chat re-keys `created_by` to the resumer** — rejected: an identity perimeter that moves when someone clicks is not a perimeter, and it silently transfers authorship of in-flight work and command of a live test Project.
- **Full creator authority over Skills and Agents from the pod**, mirroring ADR-0062's philosophy exactly — rejected: it lets one injected instruction delete the Organization's Skill catalogue, and ADR-0062's own containment argument depends on Projects being cheap, which Skill Versions are not.
- **Ownership-scoping artifacts to the chat that created them** — rejected: it breaks the "Edit with Builder" round trip by construction, since that deep link seeds a *new* chat on an existing artifact.
- **In-chat publish to other Environments, and in-chat widen-to-public** — rejected for the same reason ADR-0056 rejected in-chat publish, sharpened by ADR-0057 making widening irreversible: the surface that renders the target Environment's current occupant, the resulting audience and the validation output is where an unrepeatable act belongs.
- **Refusing to delete a chat holding unpublished work** — rejected: it protects an abandoned draft at the cost of an unprunable list, and archive already preserves the work for anyone who wants it.
- **A helper CLI wrapping the test loop** — rejected by the owner. It would have bought persisted ids across context loss, one blocking wait instead of a poll per turn, and the one-live invariant enforced structurally rather than remembered; each is recoverable in documented shell, and the CLI would couple the Builder's `version` and the agent image tag to every change in ADR-0062's allow-list.

## Consequences

- **Supersedes [ADR-0056](0056-builder-chats-are-hidden-projects-with-writes-resolved-to-the-launching-user.md) entirely.** Its name and slug, one-profile-both-kinds shape, hidden-Project carrier, gateway-key-resolved-to-creator credential, dual door, agent-pulled edit round trip, and `poolSize` 0 anatomy are restated above; its `gateway/store` controllers, creator-only listing, draft-tier power boundary and serial-roleplay testing are replaced. Its testing clause was already retired by [ADR-0063](0063-the-builder-tests-a-child-agent-by-conversing-with-it-in-one-throwaway-project.md).
- **Answers the clause [ADR-0059](0059-skills-exist-only-as-published-versions-and-a-built-in-agents-skills-live-on-a-development-profile.md) left here**: a private artifact's unfinished work is creator-only because the chat holding it is, and losing a chat is guarded by archive rather than by a draft row.
- **Schema**: `projects` gains `kind` (nullable, `builder_chat | test`) alongside the `created_by` and `created_by_project_id` of ADR-0056 and ADR-0062, none of which exist yet; `skills` and `agents` each gain a nullable `created_in_chat_project_id`.
- **`AgentRegistryEntry` grows beyond `poolSize`/`version`/`model`/`variant`** for the first time — ADR-0062's per-Agent platform-API switch lands on it, and the Builder is the launch holder of that flag.
- **Four listing sites, not one, must learn the `kind` predicate**, and a fifth that forgets it shows every author's chats and test Projects to the whole Organization. This is the concrete form of the hazard ADR-0063 flagged.
- **The Agent picker excludes `skill-agent-builder`**, extending [ADR-0054](0054-built-in-and-custom-agents-coexist-by-scope.md)'s liveness rules by one exclusion, since Builder chats are only ever created from the Store surface.
- Storage attribution is unchanged: Builder chats and test Projects are tenant Projects and appear under their tenant in Admin storage, which attributes by `projects.tenant_id` and `projects.title`.
- Glossary: **Skill & Agent Builder** and **Builder chat** stand; **chat kind** is added; "Builder Project" stays retired.
- **Accepted gaps**: a chat's transcript is unreadable by anyone but its creator, so the record others see is the artifacts, not the reasoning; and nothing caps how many chats or archived chats an Organization accumulates, which belongs with the map's standing quota fog, alongside the absence of any rate limiting or storage-quota enforcement in the backend.
